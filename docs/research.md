# Target choice: ReconArt

Checked 6 November 2026. Family: transaction-reconciliation. Daily two-sided transaction matching is distinct from the existing month-end close build's account certification and from supplier statement reconciliation. The existing Preservica family is digital preservation and was excluded for this run.

Annual bill basis: a reseller lists US$1,700 per month, annualised to US$20,400. This is indicative third-party pricing, not a verified invoice, current vendor quote or measured NZ/AU small-business spend. ReconArt's current plans page quotes edition-based annual subscriptions privately. No reseller figure appears on the public comparison page.
